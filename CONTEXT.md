# Earth Online

Earth Online is a local-first life-recording experience expressed through the fiction that Earth is a continuously running server. Its product language separates what creates short-video fascination from what remains meaningful in repeated use.

## Language

**传播爆点**:
The signature moment that makes a short-video viewer stop within roughly three seconds and understand why Earth Online is unlike a normal web app. It must arise from a real product interaction rather than a recording-only effect.
_Avoid_: 炫技动画, 开屏特效, 视觉包装

**真实闭环**:
The usable path that lets a player enter, leave a truthful personal record, and later return to find that record still meaningful. It prevents the传播爆点 from becoming an empty one-time demonstration.
_Avoid_: 功能堆砌, 完整后台, 留存机制

**爆火目标**:
The first-stage priority of maximizing short-video stopping power and sharing while preserving the真实闭环. When these goals conflict during the first stage,传播爆点 receives priority unless it would make the product dishonest or non-repeatable.
_Avoid_: 单纯好看, 功能完整, 保证爆火

**概念红利**:
The existing audience familiarity created by popular Earth Online images and videos. The product should spend this familiarity on surprise rather than explaining the premise again.
_Avoid_: 世界观科普, 概念介绍

**交互证据**:
A visible, immediate causal response from the Earth to a player's input that proves the work is interactive rather than another generated image or concept video.
_Avoid_: 自动播放演示, 纯动效, 假操作

**产品核心**:
A real local Earth Online client that turns the scattered Chinese-internet Earth Online meme into something a player can actually log into, establish a save in, receive truthful broadcasts from, and inhabit through a real signal anchor on Earth.
_Avoid_: 游戏化任务工具, 地球展示器, 概念落地页

**人生存档成就**:
A player-confirmed interpretation of a real past experience as an Earth Online achievement. It gives identity and retrospective meaning without claiming to objectively judge the player's life.
_Avoid_: 奖励徽章, AI 裁决, 抽奖收藏品

**每日任务**:
A small, optional translation of present-day life into an Earth Online action. It should invite movement without turning life into compulsory productivity or a conventional task manager.
_Avoid_: 待办事项, 打卡指标, 强制签到

**传播内容**:
The life-achievement experience is the strongest current candidate for giving a viewer a personalized, recognizable result worth sharing. It travels through the client model rather than defining the client structure.
_Avoid_: 功能总览, 每日任务列表

**日常内容**:
The daily-task loop gives a player a lightweight reason to return and can create truthful records from which later achievements emerge. It is content delivered by Earth Online, not a task-management architecture.
_Avoid_: 签到留存, 日活机制, 任务中心

**玩家存档**:
The local, persistent representation of one player's presence and history in Earth Online. It is read or recovered during connection and remains under the player's control.
_Avoid_: 账号, 云端角色, 用户画像

**玩家信号锚点**:
The player's confirmed presence at a real city-scale location on the Earth. It is the causal origin and return point for system communication, not decorative map data.
_Avoid_: 定位图钉, 城市打卡, 在线用户点

**系统播报**:
One truthful, context-resolved message sent from the player's signal anchor after connection. It reports actual local state and never exists merely to fill an interface.
_Avoid_: 消息流, 公告列表, 自动轮播

**安静运行态**:
The default post-broadcast state in which Earth remains primary, the client demands no continuous operation, and optional communication can be opened through the player's anchor.
_Avoid_: 精简面板, 空白首页, 功能导航

**通信通道**:
An on-demand interaction opened through the player's signal anchor for accessing a specific Earth Online capability without exposing a permanent dashboard or global navigation.
_Avoid_: 菜单, Tab, 功能卡片

**旧存档扫描**:
The achievement-discovery interaction in which the system presents one plausible past-life record at a time and the player chooses whether to recover it. The system proposes; the player remains the authority.
_Avoid_: 成就问卷, 图鉴勾选, 自动判定

**首次扫描节奏**:
The three-beat emotional order for the opening old-save scan: humorous recognition, unexpected validation, then meaningful self-recognition. Candidate records may change, but the emotional progression remains stable.
_Avoid_: 随机三连, 连续煽情, 全程玩梗

**开场扫描**:
The first three recovered old-save records that complete the首次扫描节奏 and unlock the rest of the experience. Three is a narrative threshold, not the total size of the achievement system.
_Avoid_: 三条成就上限, 完整问卷

**深度扫描**:
An optional continuation of old-save recovery that advances one candidate signal at a time only when the player asks to continue. It persists progress and can be left at any moment.
_Avoid_: 自动连播, 强制做完, 成就瀑布流

**生涯成就**:
An achievement that gives lasting meaning to a real milestone, sustained effort, relationship, recovery, or personal turning point.
_Avoid_: 里程碑记录, 正向奖励

**隐藏成就**:
A player-confirmed humorous or self-aware achievement triggered by an accidental, ironic, or highly relatable life pattern. It remains separate from representative titles and level evaluation.
_Avoid_: 彩蛋记录, 异常诊断, 负面标签
